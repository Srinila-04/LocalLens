PRACTICAL GUIDELINES

Before You Go

Thoughtful preparation ensures your journey remains safe, memorable, and kind to the environment.

Check Local Weather

Monsoon rains can quickly transform Ghat road conditions. Always review regional weather advisories before heading out.

Respect Local Communities

Dress respectfully in village settings, ask before taking photographs, and support native businesses and artisans.

Carry Reusable Water Bottles

Single-use plastics threaten remote hills and streams. Refill your durable bottles at verified clean checkpoints.

Avoid Littering

Leave no trace behind. Carry your wrappers and waste back to municipal collection bins in primary towns.

Follow Forest Restrictions

Respect wildlife sanctuary checkpoints, stay strictly on designated trails, and never venture into restricted zones after dark.

Travel Responsibly

Embrace patience, minimize loud noise, and keep your footprint small so these untouched sanctuaries stay pristine for generations.
